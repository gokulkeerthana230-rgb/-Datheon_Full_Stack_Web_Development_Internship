Full System Breakdown

Staff Automation System

PHP, MySQL, HTML5, CSS3 & JavaScript Architecture

Project Summary

The Staff Automation System is a centralized web application created to simplify and digitize administrative processes across collegiate academic departments. Instead of manual filing, all staff data is categorized, secured, and easily retrievable by administrative staff.

Core Modules & Implementation Details

1. Staff Profile & Demographics

Stores faculty personal info, official email, contact number, blood group, emergency contacts, date of joining, and department mapping.

2. Academic Qualifications

Detailed records of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctorate degrees, granting institutions, passing years, and specialized research disciplines.

3. Professional Experience

Records past teaching, research, and industrial tenures, current designations, promotions, and accumulated service duration.

4. Publications & Research Activities

Tracks national and international journal papers, ISBN/ISSN details, conference proceedings, workshops attended, and patent filings.

5. Automated Reports & Export Module

Generates instant summary reports filtered by department, experience level, or publication count. Formatted for academic audits, NAAC/NIRF reporting, and administrative review.

Relational Database Design (MySQL)

tbl_staff_master PK: staff_id
FK: dept_id
tbl_qualifications PK: qual_id
FK: staff_id
tbl_publications PK: pub_id
FK: staff_id

All tables are structured up to 3rd Normal Form (3NF) to guarantee referential integrity and eliminate redundancy.
